## BFAR / Department of Agriculture

- Official logo page: https://www.bfar.da.gov.ph/bfar-official-logo/
- Original download linked by that page: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1o1L68re7Pmi7x5i5zOhAcDyvx0IwRpzx/view
- Local asset: `bfar-philippines.jpg`, original 3508 x 2480 JPEG; retrieved 28 October 2026
- SHA-256: `b06466cdb1eadf3ce3f2eeda72c334dd03d56f7f1f614950e38142070e09f264`

The original paired DA/BFAR mark has a white background. Preserve its colors,
full artwork and proportions; display it on a white badge with object-fit contain.
`bfar-horizontal.svg` is a project-specific horizontal credit layout requested
by the owner, not a replacement official logo. It embeds the original JPEG
bytes without altering the emblem, excludes only blank white margins, and adds
BFAR and its full agency name to the right. The built-in image editor supplied
the layout study; the final SVG composes the original artwork with live text
to retain the exact mark and spelling. The original JPEG remains unchanged.

The project owner confirmed DICT collaboration and requested BFAR beside DICT
in this local closing credit. A separate ICT organization and its logo remain
deferred until the owner identifies them.
